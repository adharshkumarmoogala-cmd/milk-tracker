Farmers. One day. Zero chemicals. 🌾

This week we hosted an exposure visit at our Bio-Input Resource Center (BRC). Farmers came with the LODI MSSS, Warangal team to see how natural farming works on a real farm.

What they learned, hands-on:
🌿 How we prepare organic kashayams: Vepa (neem), NIK, NFK, NPK & MNM
🪱 Our vermicompost unit, from cow dung and crop waste to black gold
🐄 Our desi-cow goshala, where every bio-input starts
🌼 A field walk through the mango orchard, marigold, intercrops and nursery

Thank you, LODI MSSS Warangal, for bringing such curious, hard-working farmers 🙏
